## English

### Your Kindle reading, supercharged

Kindle highlights should not disappear into an archive after you finish a book. Vocabulary should stay connected to the sentence where you found it. Notes should remain searchable, editable, portable, and ready to review.

**KMate** is a privacy-first Kindle notes and vocabulary manager for **macOS, Windows, iPhone, and iPad**. It is the all-new successor to Kindle Mate, the classic tool trusted by Kindle readers for more than a decade.

KMate brings the whole reading-to-learning workflow together: import highlights, notes, and Vocabulary Builder words; organize and deduplicate them; revisit them in Reading Mode or flashcards; add local, online, or AI-assisted definitions; track progress; and export your data in open, useful formats.

### Why KMate

- **One home for your Kindle knowledge:** manage highlights, related notes, book notes, and vocabulary together instead of splitting them across separate tools.
- **Every major Kindle source:** import from Kindle devices, the Kindle app, Amazon Kindle Cloud, and KMate databases.
- **Desktop depth, mobile continuity:** organize and enrich your library on Mac or Windows, then review notes and words on iPhone or iPad.
- **Your data stays yours:** local-first storage, personal iCloud sync on supported Apple devices, and flexible exports with no forced third-party knowledge base.

### Key features

#### Unified Kindle import and smart organization

- Import from all Kindle models, including Paperwhite, Colorsoft, Scribe, Oasis, Voyage, and Touch.
- Bring in highlights and notes from Kindle devices, the Kindle app, and Amazon Kindle Cloud.
- Automatically recognize different languages, time zones, books, authors, sources, and color marks.
- Merge repeated imports and filter duplicate highlights, similar clippings, and Kindle Scribe auto-save clutter.
- Organize by book, author, year, language, source, tag, color, date, and reading or learning status.
- Search across large libraries with full-text and wildcard search.

#### Vocabulary Builder, dictionaries, and AI definitions

- Import Kindle Vocabulary Builder words with their original usage sentences and frequency.
- Keep words connected to source books, context, definitions, and learning progress.
- Use **MDict/MDX, StarDict, MOBI, and custom text dictionaries**, including rich HTML/CSS definitions and pronunciation where available.
- Compare multiple dictionary results with Google/Bing translation and supported AI definition providers such as Gemini and DeepSeek.
- Sort and filter by frequency, date, word level, source book, and learning status.

#### Reading Mode and knowledge review

- Revisit highlights, notes, and vocabulary in immersive card, list, or article-style views.
- Edit with Markdown, link notes to highlights, and add independent book notes, thoughts, or reviews.
- Customize themes, fonts, zoom, card colors, and Read/Unread or Learning/Mastered status.
- Use activity calendars, heatmaps, and reading statistics to understand when, what, and how much you read and learn.

#### Flexible export and Anki workflow

- Export selected books, date ranges, fields, notes, or vocabulary to **Markdown, PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, and TXT**.
- Create **Anki decks** for spaced-repetition study.
- Customize Anki front/back fields, card styles, layouts, dictionary content, and original HTML.
- Copy, print, share, or move your reading data into tools such as Obsidian and Notion.

### KMate Mobile for iPhone and iPad

Turn short breaks into useful review sessions:

- Import Kindle highlights, notes, and vocabulary from the Kindle app or Amazon Cloud directly on mobile.
- Import a KMate desktop database with one tap.
- Sync highlights, notes, vocabulary, definitions, tags, and learning progress between Mac, iPhone, and iPad through your personal iCloud account.
- Browse by book cover or card, search and edit content, and create Markdown notes.
- Study with random or progressive flashcards and quick learning-status controls.
- View statistics and export or share as TXT, Markdown, PDF, or Anki decks, depending on your plan.

### Mac, Windows, and iOS together

| Platform | Best for | Continuity |

| **iPhone & iPad** | Mobile import, reading, editing, flashcards, quick review, statistics, and sharing | iCloud sync with Mac or database import from desktop |

### Privacy and data ownership

KMate is designed around local-first reading and learning. Your library is stored on your device, and KMate does not require you to upload it to a third-party knowledge service. Network access is used only for features you choose, such as Amazon Cloud import, online dictionary lookup, AI definitions, or personal iCloud sync.

Your data remains portable: export it whenever you need, in formats that work beyond KMate.

### Free and Pro

- **Mac:** Free is fully functional with no time limit and displays your 1,000 most recent imported items. Unlock unlimited capacity with a one-time purchase or subscription.
- **Windows:** the full Pro edition includes unlimited capacity and a 15-day free trial. A one-time purchase unlocks lifetime use; there is no subscription.
- **iPhone & iPad:** start free with Kindle/Amazon imports, browsing, flashcards, TXT export, and Anki export. Pro adds iCloud sync, premium reading customization, Markdown/PDF export, printing, and unlimited Stats & Insights.

Store offerings can change. Check the relevant store page for current availability, pricing, and purchase options.

## 简体中文

### 让你的 Kindle 阅读积累真正流动起来

读完一本书后，标注不该沉睡在归档里；生词不该脱离最初遇见它的语境；笔记也应该始终可以搜索、编辑、复习和自由导出。

**KMate** 是一款面向 **macOS、Windows、iPhone 与 iPad** 的本地优先 Kindle 笔记和生词管理工具，也是经典 Kindle Mate 的全新一代产品。十余年来，Kindle Mate 一直陪伴世界各地的 Kindle 读者整理阅读积累。

KMate 把阅读到学习的完整流程连接在一起：导入 Kindle 标注、笔记与生词本，统一整理和智能去重，通过阅读模式或闪卡随时复习，补充本地词典、在线翻译与 AI 释义，查看学习统计，并将数据导出为开放、实用的格式。

### 为什么选择 KMate

- **一处管理全部 Kindle 知识：** 统一管理标注、关联笔记、书籍笔记与生词，不必在多个单一功能工具之间来回切换。
- **覆盖主要 Kindle 数据来源：** 支持 Kindle 设备、Kindle App、Amazon Kindle Cloud 与 KMate 数据库。
- **桌面深度管理，移动随时复习：** 在 Mac 或 Windows 上整理和完善资料，在 iPhone 或 iPad 上利用碎片时间继续阅读与学习。
- **数据由你掌控：** 本地优先存储，Apple 设备间可使用个人 iCloud 同步，并可自由导出，不强制绑定第三方知识库。

### 主要功能

#### Kindle 多源导入与智能整理

- 支持所有 Kindle 型号，包括 Paperwhite、Colorsoft、Scribe、Oasis、Voyage 与 Touch。
- 从 Kindle 设备、Kindle App 和 Amazon Kindle Cloud 导入标注与笔记。
- 自动识别不同语言、时区、书籍、作者、来源与颜色标记。
- 重复导入时智能合并，并可筛查重复标注、相似摘录及 Kindle Scribe 自动保存产生的冗余内容。
- 按书籍、作者、年份、语言、来源、标签、颜色、日期以及阅读或学习状态整理。
- 在大型资料库中进行全文与通配符搜索。

#### Kindle 生词本、词典与 AI 释义

- 导入 Kindle Vocabulary Builder 生词，保留原始语境例句与出现频率。
- 将单词与来源书籍、上下文、释义和学习进度始终关联。
- 支持 **MDict/MDX、StarDict、MOBI 与自定义文本词典**，并可显示词典中的 HTML/CSS 样式与发音。
- 并列查看多个词典结果，使用 Google/Bing 翻译，以及 Gemini、DeepSeek、ChatGPT 等支持的 AI 释义服务。
- 按词频、日期、词汇等级、来源书籍与学习状态排序和筛选。

#### 阅读模式与知识复习

- 通过沉浸式卡片、列表或文章视图重读标注、笔记与生词。
- 使用 Markdown 编辑内容，将笔记与标注关联，或为每本书独立记录想法、书评和读书笔记。
- 自定义主题、字体、缩放、卡片颜色，以及已读/未读、学习中/已掌握等状态。
- 通过活动日历、热力图与阅读统计，了解自己在何时阅读、读了什么，以及积累和掌握了多少内容。

#### 灵活导出与 Anki 学习流程

- 按书籍、时间范围、字段或内容类型，将笔记与生词导出为 **Markdown、PDF、Word、Excel、CSV 与 TXT**。
- 生成 **Anki 牌组**，直接进行间隔重复学习。
- 自定义 Anki 正反面字段、卡片样式、排版、词典内容，并可保留原始 HTML。
- 复制、打印或分享内容，轻松衔接 Obsidian、Notion 等工具。

### iPhone 与 iPad 版 KMate Mobile

把碎片时间变成真正有效的复习：

- 直接在移动端从 Kindle App 或 Amazon Cloud 导入 Kindle 标注、笔记与生词。
- 一键导入 KMate 桌面端数据库。
- 通过个人 iCloud，在 Mac、iPhone 与 iPad 之间同步标注、笔记、生词、释义、标签与学习进度。
- 通过书籍封面或卡片视图浏览，搜索和编辑内容，并创建 Markdown 笔记。
- 使用随机或渐进式闪卡复习，通过快捷操作更新学习状态。
- 查看统计数据，并根据所用方案导出或分享 TXT、Markdown、PDF 与 Anki 牌组。

### Mac、Windows 与 iOS 协同

| 平台 | 适合场景 | 数据衔接 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **macOS** | 完整资料库管理、Kindle 导入、词典与 AI 释义、阅读模式、统计和导出 | 通过个人 iCloud 与 iPhone、iPad 同步 |
| **Windows** | 完整桌面工作流、Kindle 设备识别、资料库管理、词典、复习和导出 | 将 KMate 数据库传输并导入移动端 |
| **iPhone 与 iPad** | 移动导入、阅读、编辑、闪卡、随时复习、统计与分享 | 与 Mac 通过 iCloud 同步，或导入桌面数据库 |

### 隐私与数据所有权

KMate 围绕“本地优先”的阅读与学习方式设计。你的资料库存储在自己的设备上，无需强制上传到第三方知识服务。只有在你主动使用 Amazon Cloud 导入、在线词典、AI 释义或个人 iCloud 同步等功能时，应用才会进行相应的网络访问。

你的数据始终可以迁移。需要时，可随时导出为能在 KMate 之外继续使用的常见格式。

- **Windows：** 下载后为全功能、无限容量的 Pro 版，包含 15 天免费试用；可一次性购买终身使用，不设订阅。
- **iPhone 与 iPad：** 免费开始使用 Kindle/Amazon 导入、无限浏览、闪卡、TXT 与 Anki 导出；Pro 解锁高级阅读外观、Markdown/PDF 导出、打印及无限统计洞察。
